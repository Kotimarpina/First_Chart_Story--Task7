# Task 7 – First Chart Story: Turning Numbers into a Narrative

## 📊 World Happiness Data Story

This project is part of my **Data Analytics Internship at Veda Technologies**.

For this task, I worked with the **World Happiness Report dataset** and used **Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib** to turn data into a simple visual story.

The main focus was not just creating charts, but understanding **which chart is suitable for a particular question** and how multiple visualizations can be connected to communicate a clear narrative.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task was to:

- Understand a dataset and identify useful variables
- Select appropriate chart types for different questions
- Create 3–4 simple and meaningful visualizations
- Add a clear takeaway for each chart
- Connect the visualizations into one overall story

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Microsoft Excel / Excel dataset

---

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses the **World Happiness Report** dataset.

The analysis focuses on:

- Life Evaluation
- Log GDP per Capita
- Social Support
- Freedom to Make Life Choices
- Country-level happiness indicators

---

## 📈 Visualizations

### 1. Top 10 Countries by Average Life Evaluation

**Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Question:**  
Which countries have the highest average reported life evaluations?

**Takeaway:**  
The chart highlights the countries with the highest average reported life evaluations in the selected dataset.

---

### 2. Economic Well-being vs Life Evaluation

**Chart Type:** Scatter Plot

**Question:**  
Is higher economic well-being associated with higher life evaluation?

**Takeaway:**  
The visualization helps examine the relationship between GDP per capita and reported life evaluation.

---

### 3. Social Support vs Life Evaluation

**Chart Type:** Scatter Plot

**Question:**  
Do countries with stronger social support tend to report higher life evaluation?

**Takeaway:**  
The chart helps explore the relationship between social support and reported life evaluation.

---

### 4. Freedom vs Life Evaluation

**Chart Type:** Scatter Plot

**Question:**  
Is freedom to make life choices associated with reported life evaluation?

**Takeaway:**  
The visualization explores the relationship between perceived freedom and reported life evaluation.

---

## 📖 Data Story

The analysis starts by identifying countries with higher reported life evaluations.

After that, the analysis looks at different factors that may be associated with happiness, including economic well-being, social support, and freedom to make life choices.

Instead of looking at happiness from only one perspective, the four visualizations provide a broader view of how different social and economic indicators relate to reported life evaluation.

---

## 🔄 Workflow

1. Loaded the World Happiness Report dataset
2. Inspected the dataset structure and columns
3. Checked missing values
4. Cleaned the required data
5. Grouped countries based on average Life Evaluation
6. Created the Top 10 countries bar chart
7. Created GDP vs Life Evaluation scatter plot
8. Created Social Support vs Life Evaluation scatter plot
9. Created Freedom vs Life Evaluation scatter plot
10. Added chart-wise takeaways
11. Connected the charts into one visual narrative
